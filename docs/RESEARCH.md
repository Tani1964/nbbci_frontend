# Church website research: what the best do, and what NBBC should take from it

Research carried out 26 November 2026 by reading the live homepages of each site (not screenshots or galleries). Fonts and colours could not be read reliably from fetched page text, so typography notes come from what the pages say and how they are structured, not from their CSS.

**Sites reviewed:** 15 read successfully. Three could not be read and are listed at the end.

---

## The reference set

### 1. Church of the Highlands: churchofthehighlands.com (USA)
- **Exceptional:** The hero is short and warm ("Welcome Home"), and a one-line mission sits right under it. "New to Highlands?" and "Plan Your Visit" are both in the first screen.
- **Patterns worth learning:** Next steps grouped by audience (Kids, Students, College). The current message is promoted as a single card, not a carousel.
- **Adapt for NBBC:** Put the first-time-visitor action in the hero, and give the mission sentence real prominence.
- **Don't copy:** The very long run of repeated promo tiles. They dilute the first screen.

### 2. Elevation Church: elevationchurch.org (USA)
- **Exceptional:** "Find the right experience for you" asks how you want to attend (campus, live stream, watch party) before telling you anything else.
- **Patterns worth learning:** A skip-to-content link, a utility bar separate from the primary navigation, and a "Watch sermon" vs "Watch full experience" split.
- **Adapt for NBBC:** Offer "in person" and "online" as equal options. NBBC streams on YouTube, Facebook and radio.
- **Don't copy:** Stacked promo carousels and a two-tier nav with 15+ items.

### 3. Life.Church: life.church (USA)
- **Exceptional:** "You're Welcome Here" is plain and human. A live "Attend Online – Starts Soon" pill in the header turns the nav into a status indicator.
- **Patterns worth learning:** Live-status pill; "Find Your Next Step" tiles (prayer, groups).
- **Adapt for NBBC:** A low-friction "Ask for prayer" step, which became the Prayer & care page.
- **Don't copy:** Hard-to-verify scale claims ("130 services every week") that don't apply to a single congregation.

### 4. Hillsong: hillsong.com (Global/Australia)
- **Exceptional:** The location finder offers practical, accessibility-minded filters (wheelchair access, parents' room, interpretation).
- **Patterns worth learning:** Treating "where is it and can I get in" as a first-class question.
- **Adapt for NBBC:** Address, directions button and map together, with call and WhatsApp next to them.
- **Don't copy:** Dense mega-menu, 19-language selector, no explicit "plan a visit" path.

### 5. Redeemer: redeemer.com (USA)
- **Exceptional:** Restraint. A one-sentence statement of purpose ("A Family of Churches and Ministries for the Good of the City") and an editorial, text-led tone.
- **Patterns worth learning:** Giving available from the header on every page.
- **Adapt for NBBC:** A persistent **Give** button in the header, and editorial serif typography for statements of identity.
- **Don't copy:** Hub-only homepage with no visitor path. The page was too long to read fully (truncated).

### 6. Passion City Church: passioncitychurch.com (USA)
- **Exceptional:** The four-part headline ("For God. For People. For the City. For the World.") gives the church a voice in one line.
- **Patterns worth learning:** Leadership presented with real photographs alongside the place they serve.
- **Adapt for NBBC:** Use the church's own words as display typography: "Raising Fruitful Disciples", W.M.I.T.E., the 2026 theme.
- **Don't copy:** Cross-promotion grid competing with the main action; sermons absent from the homepage.

### 7. The Potter's House: thepottershouse.org (USA)
- **Exceptional:** Service times and address appear in the "Plan Your Visit" block, not buried on a contact page.
- **Patterns worth learning:** A four-step "Next Steps" row; a service-time strip.
- **Adapt for NBBC:** A "This Sunday" strip fixed to the bottom of the hero: 8:00 Pre-Worship, 8:30 Sunday School, 9:30 Worship.
- **Don't copy:** Heavy all-caps headlines; a staging-host link left on production.

### 8. Saddleback Church: saddleback.com (USA)
- **Exceptional:** Intent-based entry points written in the visitor's voice ("I want to get baptized", "I want to find community").
- **Patterns worth learning:** The latest message leads, with speaker and date.
- **Adapt for NBBC:** Lead the media section with the latest real recording and its date.
- **Don't copy:** Cards that link to `javascript:;` and template placeholders leaking into the page. Both are reminders to test every link.

### 9. Gateway Church: gateway.church (USA)
- **Exceptional:** "You belong here." plus campus cards that include service times. Sermon cards show speaker and runtime.
- **Patterns worth learning:** Metadata on media cards (date, type).
- **Adapt for NBBC:** Service archive cards with date and service type, with filters for Sunday, Midweek, Prayer and Special.
- **Don't copy:** 20+ ministry links in the nav. A stray "Error" string in the markup.

### 10. VOUS Church: vouschurch.com (USA)
- **Exceptional:** A "Need Care?" entry in the navigation routes prayer and support requests in one step.
- **Patterns worth learning:** Care as a navigation-level concern.
- **Adapt for NBBC:** "Prayer & care" combines the existing prayer-request and counselling forms on one page, linked from the menu and the homepage.
- **Don't copy:** Sending forms and sermons off to third-party sites, which breaks the experience. NBBC keeps its forms on-site through Formspree.

### 11. Transformation Church: transformchurch.us (USA)
- **Exceptional:** Service time ("Sundays 10:00 AM") in the hero, and a "Watch latest sermon" module with series and speaker.
- **Patterns worth learning:** Age-banded ministry descriptions.
- **Adapt for NBBC:** Service time in the hero copy itself.
- **Don't copy:** Inconsistent headline casing; repeated service time.

### 12. Celebration Church: celebration.org (USA, Jacksonville)
- **Exceptional:** Explicit response-time promises on care requests. Low-friction "I want to follow Jesus" and prayer tiles.
- **Patterns worth learning:** Honest expectations around forms.
- **Adapt for NBBC:** Clear success states on every form ("Your request has reached our prayer team"). We did not invent a response-time promise; see open questions.
- **Don't copy:** Twenty long doctrine statements on the homepage; a stale seasonal banner. NBBC's Events page hides past dates automatically for this reason.

### 13. C3 Church Global: c3churchglobal.com (Australia, global hub)
- **Exceptional:** A three-part identity line ("Christ-Centred | Spirit-Powered | Connect-Driven").
- **Patterns worth learning:** A "New to Faith" path.
- **Adapt for NBBC:** The five pillars (Evangelism, Worship, Fellowship, Discipleship, Ministry) as a compact numbered list.
- **Don't copy:** No service times or location on the homepage.

### 14. Rhema Bible Church: rhema.co.za (South Africa)
- **Exceptional:** Service times listed with their time zone and broadcast channel. "Take the next step" trio: About, Plan Your Visit, Next Steps.
- **Patterns worth learning:** Be precise about time and channel for online viewers.
- **Adapt for NBBC:** Midweek and Sunday stream times stated on the Media page; virtual prayer meetings labelled with a direct "Join online" link.
- **Don't copy:** Emoji as interface icons (the old NBBC ticker did this too); Squarespace folder-style mobile menu.

### 15. House on the Rock: houseontherock.org.ng (Nigeria)
- **Exceptional:** A sermon-first hero ("Watch Full Service") and Give in the header. Direct and practical for a Lagos audience that often watches online.
- **Patterns worth learning:** "Watch" as a primary action.
- **Adapt for NBBC:** "Watch a service" as the secondary hero action.
- **Don't copy:** A text-only site with a "being updated" banner and a "Home Draft" page title. Authentic photography matters.

### Could not be reviewed
- **Holy Trinity Brompton (UK):** htb.org returned HTTP 403 to automated reading.
- **The Meeting House (Canada):** the domain did not resolve.
- **Daystar Christian Centre (Nigeria):** only a one-line stub was returned. House on the Rock was used instead.

## What the research decided for NBBC

1. **Answer "when and where" in the first screen.** The best sites put service time and place in or right under the hero. NBBC's hero ends in a "This Sunday" strip with the three morning gatherings.
2. **Real people, real room.** Every reference that felt warm used its own congregation. NBBC's hero is the church's own drone-view photograph of Sunday worship, not stock.
3. **The church's own words as the design.** Passion City and Redeemer show that confident typography beats decoration. NBBC's distinctive material is the slogan (John 15:16), the W.M.I.T.E. mission acronym and the 2026 theme ("Exponential Growth", Psalm 115:14). Each became a typographic moment.
4. **Give and Watch everywhere, without shouting.** A Give button in the header (Redeemer, House on the Rock); "Watch a service" as the hero's second action.
5. **Care as a first-class path.** Prayer and counselling get their own page (VOUS, Life.Church).
6. **Media with metadata.** Real recordings, dated and filterable (Gateway, Saddleback), plus the live uploads playlist so the newest service is always one click away.
7. **Nothing stale, nothing fake.** Several reference sites leaked stale promos, staging links or template placeholders. NBBC's programme hides past dates automatically and shows an honest empty state.

## The original direction this produced

**"Ikorodu editorial."** A warm ivory page, aubergine-black ink, and plum taken from the church crest, with ochre drawn from the gold in Sunday aso-ebi and choir robes. Headlines are set in a literary serif (Newsreader) with italic for emphasis; UI text uses a clean grotesque (Instrument Sans). The one signature shape is the **arch**, the outline of a church window, used only for portraits and feature images. Lists and schedules are typeset as rows with hairline rules rather than stacks of rounded cards. Deep aubergine sections give the page rhythm and carry the moments of scripture.
